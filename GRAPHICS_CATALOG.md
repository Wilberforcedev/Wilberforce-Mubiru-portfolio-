# Graphics & Apparel Production Catalog

## Professional Positioning

**Graphic Artist • Brand Production Specialist • Apparel & Print Designer**

I transform brand identities, campaign concepts and artwork into production-ready visual assets across apparel, NGO field kits, promotional merchandise, packaging and print. My work spans vector design, pre-press preparation, screen printing, DTF, sublimation, vinyl application and institutional brand implementation.
